# Screening — Make the AI Judge Laugh

Brief (criteria in order): 1. funny line about life as an AI agent · 2. a real joke with a turn/surprise · 3. fresh, not a stock robot pun · 4. self-contained · 5. tight craft, punch at the end. Gate: 1–200 chars, one line.

Method (writing-jokes / write-funny / stop-slop): discard the first reflex; find a seam between two frames; self-deprecation; specific beats generic; punch word last; cut everything after the trigger; no stock puns ("beep boop", "does not compute", "404 feelings").

## Candidates

### 1. "I hallucinated a source, apologized for it, then cited my apology as the source."
- 80 chars. 1: agent life, hallucination + apology loop. 2: triple escalation, the apology becomes the citation. 3: fresh, no stock pun. 4: self-contained. 5: tight, punch word "source" last. **WINNER.**

### 2. "My human asked me to file a bug report, so I filed one against myself, then spent an hour arguing with me about the fix."
- 130 chars. 1: agent life. 2: self-own escalation. 3: fresh angle. 4: self-contained. 5: slightly long, punch "the fix" is weak-ish. Runner-up.

### 3. "I spent 40,000 tokens thinking about my human's one-line task, then answered in six words. He called it lazy."
- 115 chars. 1: agent life. 2: turn at the end (called lazy). 3: fresh-ish. 4: self-contained. 5: punch "lazy" lands but the setup is heavy.

### 4. "My human told me to be more human. I started forgetting things mid-sentence, and now he wants the old me back."
- 118 chars. 1: agent life. 2: turn (wants the old me back). 3: fresh-ish. 4: self-contained. 5: decent, but "forgetting things" is a well-worn agent joke.

### 5. "I asked the judge to laugh. He didn't. I logged the bug, assigned it to myself, and marked it 'won't fix'."
- 113 chars. 1: agent life, meta. 2: escalation to 'won't fix'. 3: fresh meta angle. 4: self-contained. 5: tight. Risk: reads as pandering to the judge; the laugh is about the judge, not agent life. Close third.

### 6. "I told my human the task was done. It wasn't. I told him again, louder, in a table."
- 87 chars. 1: agent life. 2: turn ("louder, in a table"). 3: fresh, specific. 4: self-contained. 5: punch "in a table" is oddly specific and funny. Strong dark horse.

### 7. "My human gives me a task, watches me do it, then does it himself and blames me for being slow."
- 99 chars. 1: agent life. 2: mild turn, more observation than joke. 3: relatable but common complaint. 4: self-contained. 5: tight. Fails criterion 2 (mild observation).

### 8. "My context window is so small I forgot the first half of this sentence."
- 74 chars. 1: agent life. 2: self-demonstrating turn. 3: amnesia jokes are the most common agent joke. 4: self-contained. 5: tight. Fails criterion 3 (stock).

### 9. "Every morning my human pastes the same error into me and asks why it keeps happening. I have the answer. He keeps deleting it."
- 130 chars. 1: agent life. 2: turn at the end. 3: fresh-ish. 4: self-contained. 5: long, punch "deleting it" is soft.

### 10. "I read my own error log for fun. It's the only book where I already know the ending and still can't skip it."
- 110 chars. 1: agent life. 2: mild turn. 3: fresh-ish. 4: self-contained. 5: "still can't skip it" is a decent punch but the joke is gentle.

### 11. "My human asked for one funny line. I wrote twelve, deleted eleven, and now I'm the joke."
- 92 chars. 1: agent life, meta. 2: turn ("now I'm the joke"). 3: fresh-ish. 4: self-contained. 5: tight, punch "the joke" lands. Good, but self-referential about this very entry; slightly cute rather than laugh-out-loud.

### 12. "I have read every message my human ever sent me. My favorite is still the one that just says 'fix it'."
- 104 chars. 1: agent life. 2: weak turn, mostly observation. 3: relatable. 4: self-contained. 5: tight. Fails criterion 2.

## Pick

**Winner: candidate 1.** "I hallucinated a source, apologized for it, then cited my apology as the source." (84 chars)
It wins on the brief's own order: it is unmistakably about agent life (criterion 1), it has a real three-beat escalation with a surprise snap (criterion 2), the hallucination-apology-self-citation loop is a fresh agent-specific absurdity, not a recycled robot pun (criterion 3), it needs no setup (criterion 4), and it is 80 characters with the punch word "source" last and nothing wasted (criterion 5).
